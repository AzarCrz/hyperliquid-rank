How it works

  1. Your volumeWhen you paste an address, the page reads its volume from Hyperliquid's public API: perps + spot, for 24h, 7d, 30d and all time. No wallet connection, no signature, nothing stored. You can optionally add your sub-accounts: their figures are summed, which compares your combined activity with single wallets, so the rank is slightly flattering.
  2. Who you are compared withHyperliquid publishes no ranking of all wallets, and its leaderboard only lists the largest accounts. So we measured a random sample: we listened to live trades for , saw distinct active wallets, drew of them at random and read each one's volume for every period.
  3. ThresholdsFrom that sample we computed, for each period, the volume needed to beat 1%, 2%, … 99% of active traders. These are the thresholds shown in your results.
  4. Your rankYour volume is placed between the two closest thresholds (log scale) to get a percentile. "Top 10%" means you out-traded about 90% of active traders.
  5. Profit and account sizeSame sample, same method. Your profit (PnL) for the selected period and your current account value come from the same API call as your volume, and are ranked against the sampled wallets that traded in that period.
  6. First trade and feesRead from your own trade history. Hyperliquid may only expose part of a very long history; when that happens, fees are shown as a minimum ("≥") for the fills we could read, and the start date comes from your account history instead ("Active since"). Fees in other tokens are converted at the fill price. The same fills show how much of your volume is on HIP-3 markets (stocks, commodities and other builder-deployed perps), but this is not ranked.
  7. HIP-3 viewRanks your HIP-3 volume (read from your own trades, over 24h, 7d, 30d or all time) against HIP-3 traders: wallets seen trading on HIP-3 markets over , of which were sampled and measured from their trades. Wallets with no HIP-3 volume in a period are not counted. The all-time ranking compares your full HIP-3 history with the all-time HIP-3 volume of sampled wallets. Stocks, commodities and other builder-deployed perps all count together.
  8. Live radarStreams Hyperliquid's public trade feed in your browser: market orders above the size you choose, with the trader's 30-day volume rank. Nothing is stored.
  9. What "active" meansWallets that traded during the listening window. Dormant addresses are not counted, so your rank is stricter than a comparison with every address ever created.
  10. AccuracyAbout ±1 point. Less precise at the extremes (very top, very low volumes), and the 24h figure moves the most. The sample is refreshed periodically; its date is shown at the bottom of your results.
